Nature, Art and Exploration

A nurturing place for children to learn and thrive

Little Tugs is a small, intimate family child care program for infants through age four. Children receive individualized attention in a caring home environment while enjoying meaningful opportunities to play, explore, create, and learn.

Our nature-influenced program includes a spacious outdoor classroom and garden, giving children daily opportunities for discovery, movement, imagination, and connection with the natural world.

Our Program

Learning through everyday experiences

Individualized, Mixed-Age Care

Our small, intimate family child care setting allows each child to receive individualized attention in a warm and caring home environment. Children from infancy through age four learn alongside one another, encouraging peer-to-peer learning, cooperation, and social development.

Creative, Hands-On Learning

Children take part in hands-on creative and educational activities, including art, sensory play, games, music, and movement. Activities are designed to support curiosity, confidence, and development through play.

Nature and Outdoor Exploration

A spacious outdoor nature classroom and garden provide daily opportunities for exploration, discovery, and active play in a peaceful, quiet neighborhood setting

About Julie
