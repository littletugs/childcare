Nature, Art and Exploration

A nurturing place for children to learn and thrive

Little Tugs is a small, intimate family child care program for infants through age four. Children receive individualized attention in a caring home environment while enjoying meaningful opportunities to play, explore, create, and learn.

Our nature-influenced program includes a spacious outdoor classroom and garden, giving children daily opportunities for discovery, movement, imagination, and connection with the natural world.

Our Program

Learning through everyday experiences

Individualized Care

A small-group setting allows each child to receive personal attention, encouragement, and care based on their individual needs and stage of development.

Mixed-Age Learning

Children from infancy through age four learn alongside one another, supporting peer-to-peer learning, cooperation, empathy, and social development.

Creative Exploration

Children participate in art, sensory play, games, music, movement, and other hands-on activities that encourage curiosity and creativity.

Outdoor Classroom

Our large outdoor nature classroom and garden provide space for daily exploration, active play, observation, and discovery.

Peaceful Setting

The program is located in a quiet East Falmouth neighborhood that offers a calm and welcoming environment for outdoor learning and play.

Developmentally Appropriate

Activities and routines are designed to support children's physical, social, emotional, creative, and cognitive development.

About the Provider

More than 25 years of early childhood experience

I have more than 25 years of experience in early childhood education, including work in both child care centers and family child care programs. I hold a bachelor's degree and a Massachusetts Department of Early Education and Care Director I qualification.

I am committed to providing a safe, enriching, and developmentally appropriate environment where every child is known, valued, and supported.
